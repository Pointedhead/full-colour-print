Business Card Printing
Questions & Answers

Whether you're launching a new business, refreshing your brand or topping up a box that's running low, these are the questions Australian business owners ask us most about custom business cards.

1. Can you design my business card for me?

Yes, and our business card design is completely free. It's what sets Full Colour Print apart from most business card companies in Australia, where you can end up paying extra for a designer or wrestling with a template yourself.

Send us your logo, business name, contact details and any colours you'd like, and we'll create a professional custom business card for you at no cost.

Already have business cards? We'll make changes to your existing design for free too, such as a new phone number, a new address, a different title or a refreshed layout, as long as you print them with us. There's no design fee and no hidden extras, so you simply pay for the printing.

2. What is the standard business card size in Australia?

The standard Australian business card size is 90 mm x 55 mm. It fits neatly into wallets, card holders and business card cases.

3. How much do business cards cost in Australia?

The price depends on quantity, paper stock and finish. Ordering more cards brings the cost per card down. Because design is free with us, you're not paying extra before you even get to printing. You can see our current business card printing prices on our website at www.full-colour-print.com.

4. How many business cards should I order?

We print 250, 500 or 1,000 business cards.

  • 250 cards is a good starting point for new businesses and sole traders.
  • 500 cards suits most small businesses that hand cards out regularly at job sites, networking events or appointments.
  • 1,000 cards gives you the best value per card, and suits teams, busy trades, real estate agents and anyone who hands out cards all the time.

If your contact details change often, start smaller. And since we can update your design for free when you reorder, it's easy to adjust next time.

5. What paper thickness is best for business cards?

Thickness is measured in GSM (grams per square metre). Most premium business cards are printed on 350gsm stock, which feels sturdy and professional. Thinner stock costs less, but thicker card tends to leave a better first impression. If you're not sure which suits your business, just ask us and we'll point you in the right direction.

6. Should I choose a matt or gloss finish?

Matt has a smooth, modern, understated look. It's easy to write on and doesn't show fingerprints.

Gloss makes colours and photos look vivid and shiny, which suits bold, colourful designs.

If you want to jot a note on the card, matt is usually the better choice.

7. Can I get full-colour printing on both sides?

Yes. Full-colour business cards can be printed on the front and back. Using the back for your services, a special offer, your social media handles or a QR code is a smart way to get more from every card.

8. What file format do I need if I want to supply my own design?

If you'd rather supply your own artwork, we need a print-ready PDF at 300 dpi in CMYK colour mode, with a 3 mm bleed (extra background that extends past the trim edge so there are no white borders after cutting). Keep important text and logos at least 3 to 5 mm inside the edge so nothing gets trimmed off.

Not sure if your file is print-ready? Don't worry. Send it through and we'll fix it for you, free of charge.

9. How long does business card printing take, and do you deliver Australia-wide?

Most orders are printed and dispatched within 2-3 business days, and we deliver Australia-wide, including Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart, Darwin, Canberra and regional areas. Delivery time after dispatch depends on your location, so allow a little extra time if you need your cards for an event or launch.

10. What should I put on a business card?

Keep it clear and uncluttered. The essentials are:

  • Your name and job title
  • Business name and logo
  • Phone number and email address
  • Website address
  • Business address or service area (if relevant)
  • ABN (some tradies and contractors like to include it)
  • Social media handles or a QR code linking to your website or booking page

Leave some white space. A crowded card is hard to read and easy to forget. And remember, our free design service means we'll lay it all out for you.

11. Are QR codes on business cards a good idea?

Yes. A QR code lets people jump straight to your website, portfolio, Google reviews or booking page, and most phones scan them in seconds. Make the code at least 2 cm x 2 cm, use strong contrast (dark code on a light background), and test it before sending to print.

12. Are business cards still worth it in the digital age?

Absolutely. A business card is a tangible reminder of you that sits in someone's wallet or on their desk long after a conversation ends. They work especially well for tradies, real estate agents, hairdressers, beauticians, mechanics, consultants and anyone meeting customers face to face. Handing over a quality card also signals that you're established and take your business seriously.

13. Can I reorder my business cards easily?

Yes. We keep your artwork on file, so reordering is simple. If your details have changed, we'll update your card for free before we reprint it. If you need exactly the same card again, we'll just reprint it.
